        C · Import a FHIR Questionnaire (SDC)
        
Standards-based or curated forms (e.g. a national or ABDM-aligned form pack) become ordinary element sets: they can be applied, mixed with proprietary sets, versioned, and exported back as a Questionnaire. Supported: groups, string/text/integer/decimal/quantity/boolean/date/dateTime, choice with inline options, item codes (SNOMED/LOINC). Not supported yet (flagged on import): conditional display, calculated expressions, value-set options, multi-answer, attachments.
